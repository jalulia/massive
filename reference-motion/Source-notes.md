## Powers of Ten edits

The excerpts preserve source speed, color and picture geometry. Full-frame exports preserve original labels. Clean variants crop the central image window, removing side captions while retaining internal scale boxes. The crop is not a calibrated physical field of view. GIFs repeat with a visible editorial reset; the scientific zoom is not a natural cycle.

Web GIFs are 640 × 480 full frame and 480 × 480 square, at 10 fps with 128 colors. MP4s retain the existing 1440 × 1080 full-frame and 960 × 960 square delivery encodes. The larger local GIF masters are retained in the project.

## Presentation use

Earth provides planetary context, not a magnetosphere mechanism. The film’s carbon atom complements the separate hydrogen orbital reference. The galaxy passages provide scale context, not cosmic-web formation. Cell and DNA excerpts do not demonstrate abiogenesis. Keep Harvard, NASA, Abbott and Illustris as the specific science references for their respective sections.
