About the sources and loop behavior
  • Powers of Ten source: 2880 × 2160, the highest-resolution full-length upload found in the checked sources. Its uploader does not document an original-film scan or restoration. It remains visibly soft; the extra pixels are not proof of new detail.
  • Web GIFs: 640 × 480 complete frames and 480 × 480 clean squares, at 10 fps. H.264 MP4s: 1440 × 1080 full frame and 960 × 960 square. The clean crop removes side scale labels; the original image and internal scale boxes remain.
  • Powers of Ten excerpts run forward at source speed and restart with a visible editorial cut. These are selected passages through scale, not naturally cyclic events. No reversed footage or invented zoom has been added.
  • Sagan’s 1080p stream is labeled AI-upscaled by YouTube. No additional enhancement was applied here.
  • Resonance, a magnetosphere demonstration, a black-hole traversal, and cosmic-web formation are not depicted by these Powers of Ten extracts. Retain Harvard, NASA, Abbott and Illustris as the specific reference sources requested in the presentation chat.
