Two new levels, authored enemy encounters, and a new Repulsor treatment in Matt’s latest version.

New level · Stage 001

LATTICE

The grid comes apart and reconnects. Loose threads retract toward their remaining anchors, leaving shifting patches of separated RGB dots.

Inside those patches, movement steps between dots in eight directions. Thrust commits to a neighbouring dot; Swipe and Repulsor hold the player’s position. Returning to connected grid restores ordinary movement.

LATTICE feature notes ↗

New level · Stage 009

COSMOS

An elliptical arena holds a cosmic web that evolves with the match: dispersed matter gathers into filaments, clusters merge, and dark voids open between them.

Localized warning glows build into supernova flashes, each ejecting a collectible mass nugglet. Their frequency follows the age of the web. COSMOS also joins Level Select with its own moving, three-dimensional cloud icon and instructions.

COSMOS feature notes ↗

Gameplay · ORBITAL

Enemies arrive in formation

ORBITAL now runs an authored encounter timeline: paired rows, rings and goal-facing phalanxes arrive with warnings and staggered entrances. Surviving enemies affect how much room remains for the next wave.

The expanded lineup includes lunging Seekers, projectile-firing Ranged Drones, wall-mounted Particle Beam Turrets, and Carriers that launch six Drones from their shells. Encounter Composer lets Matt arrange and preview these arrivals.

Encounter notes ↗

Combat · Third melee stage

Repulsor, rebuilt

The combo’s radial finish now has an inward charge, a bright source flash, an expanding pressure front and a turbulent blast shell. The grid bends with the pulse.

Charge follows the player; the released blast stays at its release point. Its active wave can damage enemies once per pulse, while the fading aftermath carries no hitbox. Shared reach and damage controls make the attack easier to tune.
